About your bus trip

Coaches are often the most affordable way to travel between cities. A few preparations make the journey more comfortable.

15 minArrive before boarding
1 + 1Checked bag plus carry-on, varies by operator
StopConfirm your exact pick-up point

Pick-up point

  • Coaches can leave from different stations or street stops in the same city.
  • Check the exact address in your confirmation email.
  • Arrive early so you don't miss the coach.

Luggage

  • Check your operator's weight and size limit before you pack.
  • Keep passport, medication and chargers in your carry-on bag.
  • Ask for a luggage tag and keep the receipt.

Comfort on board

  • Bring a neck pillow, water and a light layer, because coaches can be cold.
  • Charge your phone before you leave, since sockets and Wi-Fi are not always available.
  • Long routes often include rest stops. Note the times in advance.
