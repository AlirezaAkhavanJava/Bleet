Before you book

  • Check whether the price includes taxes and service fees.
  • Look for free cancellation, and note the deadline.
  • Confirm whether breakfast is included or charged separately.

At check-in

  • Bring your booking reference and a photo ID.
  • Some hotels place a deposit on a card, so keep it available.
  • Arriving early? Ask whether your room is ready, or leave your bags at reception.

Choosing the area

  • Check the distance to the nearest metro or bus stop on a map.
  • Read recent reviews that mention noise, cleanliness and location.
  • Save the hotel address offline, in case your phone loses signal.
