About your train trip

Rail is one of the easiest ways to travel between cities. These tips help you board smoothly and avoid last-minute stress.

20 minArrive before departure
3Ticket classes
IDCarry it when travelling

Before you board

  • Check the platform on the departure board, as it can change.
  • Have your ticket or booking reference ready on your phone or on paper.
  • Allow extra time at busy stations.

On the train

  • Check your seat number and carriage before you sit down.
  • Keep valuables and small bags with you, not on the rack.
  • Business and First class usually include more space and power sockets.

If you change trains

  • Leave at least 20 minutes between connections when possible.
  • Check whether the next train is on the same platform or a different one.
  • Keep every ticket until you reach your final stop.
