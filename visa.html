About your visa application

Requirements differ by country and by your nationality. Use this page as a checklist, then confirm everything with the destination's embassy or consulate before you apply.

Documents

  • A passport with enough blank pages, and in good condition.
  • Passport-style photos that meet the country's size and background rules.
  • Proof of travel, accommodation and funds. Requirements vary by visa type.

Timing

  • Appointment slots can be booked out for weeks, so start early.
  • Processing times in the results are estimates, not guarantees.
  • Keep a copy of every document you submit.

Avoid scams

  • Apply only through official government or embassy websites.
  • Be wary of agencies that ask for payment outside official channels.
  • Never share your passport details with anyone who contacts you unprompted.
