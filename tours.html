About your tour

Guided tours are a great way to see a city in a short time. Dress for the weather, arrive on time and check what's included.

10 minArrive before the meeting time
SmallGroup sizes vary, check before booking
GuideCheck the language they speak

What to bring

  • Comfortable walking shoes, because most tours cover a lot of ground.
  • Water, sunscreen, a hat and a light waterproof layer.
  • Your ID, and cash or a card for extras such as snacks or entry tickets.

Meeting point

  • Check whether pick-up from your hotel is included.
  • Find the meeting point the day before, and note the guide's contact details.
  • If you're late, contact the operator straight away.

Before booking

  • Check the cancellation rules and the refund deadline.
  • Ask about accessibility if anyone needs step-free routes or rest stops.
  • Read whether tickets, meals and transport are included in the price.
